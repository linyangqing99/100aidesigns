Frequently Asked Questions

ForgeGUI 是什么？适合谁？

Yes. You can start free, try the creator tools, and upgrade when you need more generations, faster workflows, or team features.
什么是积分？如何使用？
Credits are used when you generate assets. Different tools use different credit amounts depending on asset type and generation complexity.

Are my ForgeGUI generations added to the public Asset Catalog?
Eligible generations made with free credits or during a free trial may be added to ForgeGUI's public Asset Catalog after you accept the catalog notice. Public assets can be viewed, downloaded, and indexed by search engines. You can stop future publication in My Account → Publish free generations. Turning the toggle off cancels pending publication, but does not automatically remove assets already published. To remove existing catalog entries, use My Account → Published assets → Remove all. Paid-plan generations are private by default unless you opt in, and you retain ownership of your generations.
